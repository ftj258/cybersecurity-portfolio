# Research

### The Case of "The Cannibal Cop"

A research paper examining the Gilberto Valle ("Cannibal Cop") case — the NYPD officer convicted (later acquitted on appeal) of conspiracy to kidnap, based on violent fantasy roleplay conversations found on his computer. The paper looks at the digital evidence involved and the legal/forensic questions it raised about distinguishing fantasy from intent in digital communications.
